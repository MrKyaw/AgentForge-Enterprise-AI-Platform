# Client Service Standards

> Synthetic reference document for the AgentForge demo. Not a real service commitment.

## Service tiers

Lumen Ridge offers three service tiers. **Essential** includes digital self-service, standard reporting, and annual reviews. **Priority** includes a named advisor, quarterly portfolio reviews, and a four-hour response target during business hours. **Institutional** includes a dedicated relationship team, monthly reviews, custom reporting, and an assigned service manager.

## Response standards

A client email must receive an initial response within **one business day**. A telephone call must be returned the same business day. A secure message receives a response within two business days. During market disruptions, the service desk publishes an updated response target on the client portal.

## Complaints

Every client complaint must be acknowledged within **two business days** of receipt. The target resolution time is **ten business days**. If resolution will take longer, the client receives a written update every five business days. A complaint unresolved after twenty business days is escalated to the Head of Client Experience and the Compliance team. All complaint records are retained for seven years.

## Vulnerable clients

Team members must follow the vulnerable-client procedure when they observe signs of diminished capacity, coercion, or financial exploitation. The procedure includes a second-person review, a documented support plan, and escalation to the Client Experience lead. The platform must never infer a vulnerability from sensitive personal data.

## Records and privacy

Client interactions are recorded in the CRM within one business day. Personal information must be limited to what is necessary for servicing. Recordings are retained according to the Records Retention Schedule. A client may request access to their personal information through the Privacy Office.

## Escalation matrix

A service failure affecting more than one client is a major incident and must be escalated to the Service Management team within thirty minutes. A complaint involving advice suitability must be routed to Compliance immediately. Any suspected fraud is reported to the Fraud Response team and the Chief Information Security Officer.

## Quality monitoring

The Client Experience team samples at least ten interactions per advisor each quarter. Quality scores cover accuracy, empathy, timeliness, and completeness. Coaching plans are created for scores below the agreed threshold.
