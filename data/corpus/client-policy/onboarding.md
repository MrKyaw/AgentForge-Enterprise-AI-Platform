# Client Onboarding Standard

> Synthetic reference document for the AgentForge demo. Not legal or compliance advice.

## Institutional onboarding

An institutional client cannot be activated until the onboarding team receives and validates the following documents: a signed investment management agreement, an **investment policy statement** (IPS), **beneficial ownership** documentation, **AML/KYC** evidence, authorized signatory list, tax forms, and custody instructions. The IPS must state the mandate, risk tolerance, liquidity needs, and any exclusion list.

The onboarding team records the legal entity name, jurisdiction, regulatory status, and ultimate beneficial owners in the client master. A sanctions and adverse-media screening is completed before activation. Any unresolved screening alert is escalated to Compliance and the account remains blocked.

## Retail onboarding

A retail client must provide government-issued identification, proof of address, a completed risk profile questionnaire, and a suitability declaration. Where a client is represented by a power of attorney, the legal authority must be validated by the Legal team. The advisor confirms the client's investment objective and time horizon before the first trade.

## Data quality

The client master is the authoritative source for legal name, addresses, tax residency, and authorized contacts. Duplicate client records must be merged by the data stewardship team. Missing mandatory fields block account activation. Changes to authorized signatories require dual approval.

## Reviews and remediation

Onboarding files are reviewed for completeness within two business days of receipt. Deficiencies are tracked to closure with an accountable owner. An account activated with a temporary exception must be remediated within thirty calendar days. Material exceptions are reported to the Client Onboarding Committee monthly.

## Privacy and retention

Only the minimum personal information required for onboarding is collected. Documents are stored in the approved repository with access controls and encryption. Retention follows the Records Retention Schedule. Access is reviewed quarterly.
