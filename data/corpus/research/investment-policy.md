# Investment Policy Standard

> Synthetic reference document for the AgentForge demo. It is not legal, compliance, or investment advice.

## Purpose and scope

This standard sets portfolio construction and monitoring requirements for discretionary mandates managed by Lumen Ridge Asset Management. It applies to the Core Equity, Balanced, and Income strategies. Portfolio managers remain accountable for every investment decision; the standard defines the boundaries within which judgment is exercised.

## Concentration limits

A single issuer may not exceed **5% of portfolio market value** at the time of purchase. A position that grows above 4.5% of portfolio market value must be reviewed at the next scheduled portfolio risk meeting. A position above 6% requires written approval from the Chief Investment Officer and a documented remediation plan within five business days. The hard ceiling for any single issuer is 6.5%, measured at month-end close.

The top ten holdings may not exceed 35% of portfolio market value. A single sector may not exceed 25% of portfolio market value without approval from the Investment Risk Committee. These limits are monitored daily by the investment risk team and escalated through the portfolio manager on-call process.

## Liquidity and cash

The Core Equity strategy must hold a cash buffer between 2% and 8% of portfolio market value. The target cash weight is 4%. A cash weight outside the permitted range requires a documented tactical allocation memo approved by the Chief Investment Officer. Positions representing more than 10% of a security's average daily trading volume require a liquidation plan.

## Derivatives and counterparties

Derivatives may be used only for hedging, efficient portfolio management, or replicating permitted exposures. Speculative leverage is prohibited. Every derivative counterparty must be approved by the Counterparty Risk Committee and monitored against collateral thresholds. Currency hedges above 20% of a portfolio's non-domestic exposure require a hedge designation memo.

## Proxy voting and stewardship

The governance team administers proxy voting under the Stewardship Policy. Portfolio managers may request an abstention when a vote creates a material conflict of interest. All votes and rationales are retained for seven years. Engagement records must identify the issuer, meeting date, topic, and outcome.

## Exceptions and escalation

Any exception to this standard requires pre-approval from the Investment Risk Committee, a named accountable owner, and a remediation date. Retroactive exceptions must be reported to the Chief Risk Officer within one business day. Recurring exceptions trigger a policy review.

## Performance information

This standard does not contain fund performance, historical return figures, or client-specific results. Performance reporting is governed by the Client Reporting Standard and must be sourced from the official performance book of record.

## Review cycle

The Investment Risk Committee reviews this standard at least annually, and whenever market structure, regulation, or strategy changes materially. The current version is effective from 1 January 2026.
